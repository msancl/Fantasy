  2. Some stadiumpublished at 19:10 BST
    19:10 BST

    England v Croatia (21:00 BST

    This evening's match takes place at the Dallas Stadium - home of the most valuable sports team in the world, NFL team the Dallas Cowboys.

    It will host more games than any other venue at the 2026 World Cup and was a contender for the final until the New Jersey Stadium was chosen.

    Located in Arlington, 17 miles west of downtown Dallas, the stadium cost $1.2bn to build, with a further $295m spent on renovations ahead of the World Cup.

    Because NFL pitches are 20m narrower than Fifa's standard dimensions, the playing field will be raised to create more space, while a temporary grass surface will also be installed.

    It has been likened to a space ship and with it's unique shape you can see why.

    Oh and it's got air conditioning. But we will still have a hydration break.

  6. New coach, new tournament...New belief?published at 19:02 BST
    19:02 BST

    England v Croatia (21:00 BST)

    England's 2026 World Cup campaign begins against Croatia in Dallas tonight.

    When they suffered Euros final heartache against Spain two years ago it felt like change was coming. And it did.

    England will be led at the tournament in the United States, Canada and Mexico by German boss Thomas Tuchel - tasked with helping the Three Lions to win their first World Cup since 1966.

    No mean feat for a nation who expects so much but hasn't had anything to celebrate for 60 years.

    Is it finally time for England to shine?

    Euro heartbreak. Gareth Southgate's no longer the one. Questions over culture. A new coach. Fall outs, make ups. A perfect World Cup qualifying campaign. Big decisions. Questions. Questions...More questions. Hot weather, storm delays, stolen gear.
